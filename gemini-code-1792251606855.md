# Project Specification: White-Label Bangladeshi E-Commerce Platform ("DeshiMart BD")

## 1. Brand Identity & White-Label Rules (CRITICAL)
- **Public Store Name (Placeholder):** `DeshiMart BD` (Keep brand name, logo text, helpline, and WhatsApp number in a single config file `/src/config/siteConfig.js` so I can rename the brand anytime in 5 seconds).
- **Strict White-Label Rule:** Our products are sourced from **B-Bazar (bbazar)** at wholesale prices and resold under our own brand. **NEVER** mention or display "B-Bazar" or wholesale prices anywhere on the public customer-facing website. Customers must experience this as an official, standalone retail platform like Daraz or Othoba.
